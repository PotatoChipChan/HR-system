How yearly bonus is calculated:
1. Employee must have a yearly Performance Review grade (A/B/C/D) and meet the minimum tenure.
2. Full Bonus = Base Salary × Grade Months (A={{ policy.grade_A_months }} months, B={{ policy.grade_B_months }}, C={{ policy.grade_C_months }}, D={{ policy.grade_D_months }}).
3. Prorated Bonus = Full Bonus × (Months Worked in {{ year }} / 12).
4. Approved bonuses are paid in the configured Payout Month (default January for CNY).
